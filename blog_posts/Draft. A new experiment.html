Draft. A new experiment

Sometimes you don't need a complicated application. Just a simple place to to write and code. So... I made one.

BY NAIVEGOBLIN · 05.11.2026 · 4 MIN READ

Meet Draft Editor.

Bwaa ❤️
Always enjoyed building little things. Draft Editor started as one of those ideas: creating a simple writing environment where what you write doesn't leave ur device. Meet Draft.

So, what exactly is it?

Draft Editor is a minimalist, browser-based text editor. The idea is pretty straightforward: open it, start writing or coding, and don't worry about having what you write on another person's computer.

It's entirely built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, making it a static page.
Aka runs **only** on ur device.

Little features, big convenience.

I'm still building new features and developing this project further, as feedback starts coming.

You can create new documents, open existing ones, save your work, and download your documents whenever you need them.

Need to find something in a longer document? There's a search and replace feature for that. You can also adjust the font size, toggle line wrapping, and switch between light and dark themes depending on your mood.

Keeping things simple.

One of the things I wanted to do was a simple useful project that allows me to learn and better my knowledge of JavaScript.

I didn't want to turn this into another half-baked app like every other notion app that tracks everything you do.

"There's no need to fix what's not broken."

That's the philosophy behind Draft Editor. It's a lightweight tool that does what it's supposed to do, without asking too much from you.

But why? Honestly? Because I could and wanted.

I like building things that other people can use and this was the perfect opportunity to do so.
It might not have all the bells with wissles but it works perfectly as intended.

Give it a try!

If you need a place to write down ideas, work on some text, code, or just fuck around. Feel free to give it a go.

Thanks for stopping by, and remember: Keep making stuff, build cool shit, learn ur own things along the way.
And more importantly enjoy ur path without worrying about the final result.
